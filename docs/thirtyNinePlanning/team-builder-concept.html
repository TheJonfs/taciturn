Team builder redesign — concept

Concept reference for the implementer. Placeholder colors/icons against the game's dark theme — the Ivalician parchment skin is a separate later pass. Ability lists are representative.

One big card. The unit's whole identity — larger portrait, complete stats including Move and Jump, name/gender/Brave/Faith — lives on one card; the class collapses to a "change" control once chosen.

One inspector, both sides. A single context box explains whatever's under consideration — an equipment candidate (stat delta vs equipped) or an ability (effect + budget fit) — fed by the same source as the tooltips and the Player's Guide.

Abilities as an accordion. Budgeted multi-select per category; one category opens to its full hoverable list at a time, the rest collapse to a summary line of picks + budget.

Equipment grouped, not dumped. A slot opens to a list grouped by type and sorted, key stats inline, with search and sort — replacing the flat creation-ordered dropdown. The lineup shows each unit's level.
