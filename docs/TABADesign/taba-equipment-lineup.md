# TABA Equipment Lineup — running reference

*Living document. The authoritative record of the TABA equipment roster as it's authored, tier by tier.
Chapter 2 = the existing Mage War lineup (validated at ~L23–27); Chapters 1 and 3 are authored outward
from it (Ch1 down, Ch3 up). This tracks **what exists, in which tier, and how it's obtained** — the
economy (costs, currency, shop bundling) comes after the roster is locked.*

## Structure & conventions
- **Three gear generations**, one per chapter; a coarse step-function in power, smoothed within-chapter by
  the stat curves and by story-gated *availability* (not every tier item is buyable from a chapter's start).
- **Availability:** `shop` (unlimited, may be story-gated to a location) · `unique` (single instance, found
  not bought) · `demoted` (a Ch2 item also stocked in Ch1). Uniques stay countable inventory (supply cap 1,
  not unit-locked).
- **Armor lanes** (not power tiers): `universal` / `Heavy` (fighter) / `Magical` (mage).
- **WP floor raised to ~4–5** at Ch1 to compress the physical TTK ratio across the chapter steps.
- **Ch1 shape:** magical-forward weapons, universal+magical armor two-deep, Heavy skeletal (one each —
  Chris is the lone early Heavy customer), heads/off-hands largely *demoted* from Ch2.

**Breadth-enabler design notes:**
- Cost is **lateral, not raw-stat** (armor-identity, not the Crown's −3 MA) so it doesn't obsolete the Ch2
  Magus Crown — the two enablers teach the same lesson (breadth pays) through *different* costs. Flavor:
  "the generalist travels light."
- **Installs a reusable engine seam:** an **equip-legality override** path (override class defaults for
  what's legal to equip). This unique is *instance one*; a future "equip anything" accessory/ability
  (mix Heavy + Magical gear) is instance two. Author the override path generally, per the
  first-instance-installs-the-capability pattern (cf. Chris's parameterized cover, Thessaly's
  unit-restricted components).

**Ch1 uniques lean fire** (Pendant + Flametongue) — deliberate: fire is the protagonist's element, and a
Chapter-1 fire-synergy discovery (Lumen + Chris) is on-theme. The breadth-enabler points elsewhere
(lateral build), so the slate isn't *only* fire.

**Logged for later (Ch2/Ch3): the depth-enabler / "specialist's implement."** A unique that scales with
single-class commitment (reads `spentInClass`) — rewards going *narrow*, pushing *against* the breadth
gradient the JP economy sets. Belongs *after* the player has internalized the default shape, so it lands
as a genuine subversion, not noise. The counterpart to Ch1's breadth-teaching enabler; together they make
the campaign's unique-arc teach the rule, then break it.

**Depth-enabler — SETTLED: Tailored Outfit** (universal body). **+75 MaxHP; +25 MaxMP; at start of turn,
recover 2 HP per 100 JP in the currently-equipped class.** (Maxed Calculator ≈ 48 HP/turn.)
- **Wall → faucet:** regen (a rate), not MaxHP (a wall) — self-limiting against focus fire by construction
  (rewards not-being-targeted; doesn't stop being-targeted). Solves the "un-killable" problem: burst/
  concentrated fire (75–200/turn at that stage) outpaces it; only chip is shrugged.
- **Class-agnostic** (sustain, not an ability-amp) → no power-location skew; every deep class benefits
  equally. Kept the fairness of the HP/MP instinct without the wall.
- **Start-of-turn trigger ties regen to Speed** → the slow Calculator regens 48 but *infrequently*
  (self-balances the scariest number); keep start-of-turn deliberately.
- **Rate = 2/100 (not 3):** the number is a chip-shrug threshold; both fold to focus fire, but 3 makes a
  unit near-immune to a *single* attacker (forces mandatory focus). 2 keeps a lone attacker relevant, is
  round/legible, and is the nerf-averse floor (buff to 3 post-playtest if weak).
- **Flat +75/+25 baseline** makes it a usable body even without depth; the regen is the depth-reward on top.
- **Depth-REWARD, not breadth-TAX** (the gentle end): a wide player's deepest class qualifies. The 2.4×
  specialist-vs-dabbler gap still creates a specialize-pull (functions as counter-incentive to the breadth
  grain). Sharpening tool in reserve if playtest shows abuse: gate on "no secondary command set equipped."
